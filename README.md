# Field Office 7: the guide

The guide and walkthrough for **Field Office 7**, a night-shift desk game about cryptids. You are an alien in a trench
coat, mistaken for the new Case Officer at a broke government field office. Every night you judge sighting reports:
debunk the hoaxes, escalate the real ones, and quietly redact the reports that are about you.

- **Read the guide:** https://minormending.github.io/field-office-7-guide/
- **Play in the browser:** https://field-office-7.pages.dev/
- **Play on Android:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.minormending.fieldoffice7
  ("Field Office 7: Cryptid Desk")

The guide covers how a shift works, each night of Act One with its memo and story reports, the Field Manual's subjects
and their tells, and how scoring and suspicion work. Spoilers are behind "Show the answer" toggles.

## About this repository

It holds **only the generated site**, served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch. Every table, picture and number in it
is generated from the game's own data by the game's build tools, and the whole site is replaced on each release, so a
hand edit here would be lost. Please do not edit these files by hand or open pull requests against them.

Found a mistake in the guide or the game? Write to goldenrod.works+fieldoffice7@gmail.com.
